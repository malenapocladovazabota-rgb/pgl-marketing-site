The case

What we started with and what we built

Before

A brand-new studio starting from zero. No followers, no ads, no booking system. Every client depended on the owner.

What we did
  • A brand people remember. We built the content around the founder and her method. People come to her, not to a generic service.
  • Ads that lead straight to a booking. Every campaign sends people to pick a time, not to a DM that goes nowhere.
  • The lead specialist, packaged. She's booked months ahead at a price above the local market, with no first-visit discounts.
  • Booking that runs itself. Confirmations, four reminders, a follow-up and review request after every visit, an alert to the owner on a low score, and win-back messages for clients who haven't returned.
  • One dashboard. Every booking tied to the ad that brought it, with cost and value. The owner checks the numbers, not the inbox.
