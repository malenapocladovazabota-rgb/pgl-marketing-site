Performance · 10%

How the performance fee is calculated

The formula

We take the revenue from completed, paid visits in the month.

We subtract the advertising spend for the same month.

Our fee is 10% of what remains.

What is excluded

Your existing patients, physician-referred appointments, and your entire current insurance-billed volume. We count only the clients marketing brought in.

Worked example, one month

Revenue from completed visits$40,000
Advertising spend−$8,000
Calculation base$32,000
Performance fee, 10%$3,200
Plus monthly management$2,000
Total for the month$5,200

Transparency

Calculated from a shared booking log you have access to. Every visit is visible with its source. Invoiced monthly against completed visits.
